# Notice

A short boxed message with a bold title: what is public (`info`), a privacy or timing caution (`warning`), a completed outcome (`success`) or something the person must act on (`danger`).

- Provide: `tone`, `title` (one sentence that stands alone), `children` (one or two sentences of detail).
- `danger` is announced to screen readers; keep it for things with a deadline, such as the maker's liveness window.
- At most one notice of each tone per screen.
